The equations you'll actually use

LSS retention model — why two runs pin down two parameters per peak:

$$\ln k = \ln k_0 - S_e(\varphi-\varphi_0)$$

Gradient retention time (linear ramp, pre-gradient period $\tau$):

$$t_R = \tau + t_0 + \frac{t_0}{b_e}\ln\!\Big[b_e\big(k_0-\tfrac{\tau}{t_0}\big)+1\Big]$$

Two-run seed (large-$k_0$ approximation — starting guess only, never the answer):

$$b_{e,1} \approx \frac{t_0\ln\beta}{t'_{R,1}-t'_{R,2}/\beta} \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad S_e \approx \frac{\ln\beta}{\varphi_{e,1}-\varphi_{e,2}}$$

Resolution (use the exact constant, not 1.178):

$$R_s = \frac{t_{R,2}-t_{R,1}}{2(\sigma_1+\sigma_2)} = 1.1774\,\frac{t_{R,2}-t_{R,1}}{W_{1/2,1}+W_{1/2,2}}$$
