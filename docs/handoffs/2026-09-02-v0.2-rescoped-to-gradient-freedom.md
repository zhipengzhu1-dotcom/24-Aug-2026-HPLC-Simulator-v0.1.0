## The engine fact that shaped the whole cycle

**The forward path is already general in φ0/φf.** `predict_retention` reads
`gradient.phi0` and `gradient.delta_phi`; `peak_width` reads `gradient.phi0`. Any `Gradient`
works today. Three things pin the candidate, none of them the model:

1. `session.py::_check_candidate` — **raises** when the candidate's φ range differs from
   scouting. Becomes a warning path, per #44.
2. `streamlit_app.py::_candidate_controls` — offers tG and hold only.
3. SPEC §4's wording, which is what (1) enforces.

`fit.py::_check_runs_are_a_scouting_pair` is **not** on that list and **stays** — the two
scouting runs sharing φ0/φf/t_init is what makes the two-run fit well-posed. Freedom is a
property of the *candidate*.

So gradient freedom is two jobs of very different size: moving both ends is a constraint
removal whose real cost is honesty, and **multi-segment is the genuinely new engine work**
(per-segment closed form, cumulative band-compression, three-way case split — re-derive from
den Uijl Eq. 7–8; AutoLC-BO is CC BY-NC-SA, read don't port).

## The research, and the one number it got wrong

[#43](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/24-Aug-2026-HPLC-Simulator-v0.1.0/issues/43)
merged as `docs/research/composition-extrapolation.md` (930 lines). Two findings drive
everything downstream:

**The calibration window has a closed form**, `Δφ_e = ln β / S_e`. Verified against the real
fit — 0.0962 / 0.0984 / 0.0951 measured against 0.0970 / 0.0987 / 0.0951 predicted. It
depends on **β and S_e only**, so *widening the scouting %B range does not widen it*;
doubling it takes β = 9. The three lab peaks are calibrated over 42.7–52.4, 52.9–62.8 and
80.7–90.2 %B — **non-overlapping**, which is the real argument for per-peak.

**Elution composition depends on the candidate only through `s* = t0·Δφ/tG`.** Consequence
for SPEC §6 diagnostic 1: it currently brackets on **tG**, and while φ is locked that is the
same test. Gradient freedom breaks the equivalence *silently* — `15→55 %B at tG 25` is inside
[15, 45] and outside the real bracket. Whether the bracket variable changes is now a fourth
sub-question on #44.

**Corrected before it reached the driver:** the research put Unknown-1 below the large-k floor
at φ0 ≈ 18 %B, from the large-k approximation, and flagged it for recomputation. The engine's
own guard does not trip until **φ0 ≈ 50 %B** (k_i = 3.7). The gap is Guillarme's criterion for
*their* approximate closed form versus this engine's Brent solve. Whether that is a real hole
in our guard is open, and noted on #44. **A subagent's flagged number is a to-do, not a
result** — this one would have gone into a bench protocol.

## Lessons worth carrying

**A research ticket can invalidate the cycle it was cut for.** #40 was scoped to inform the
map's design. It found that no open-source project draws this map — hplcsim's spec is ahead
of the field — *and* that nothing anywhere publishes a resolution-map answer to validate
against. Ahead of the field and unvalidatable is a reason to wait. The ticket that was meant
to sharpen the work is what stopped it, and that was the right outcome.

**Prototype against the real data as well as the good data.** The #31 prototype shipped a
dataset switcher for exactly this reason: eight invented compounds made the panes judgeable,
and the real three-peak set made the *problem* visible. Only the second one changed the plan.

**Verify a subagent's own caveats.** See the 18 vs 50 %B correction above.
