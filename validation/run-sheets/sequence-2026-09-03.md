## 0 · Before the first injection: bring re-equilibration up to the protocol

`PROTOCOL.md` asks for ≥ 10 column volumes between runs. Every run on file re-equilibrated
for 3.4–4.0 min at 0.4 mL/min, which is 5.7–6.7 CV. The re-equilibration end-point is one
of the two surviving candidate mechanisms for the φ0 residual, so the sequence must meet
the protocol before any new φ0 run is scored.

| t0 taken as | 1 CV | 10 CV | hold needed at 0.4 mL/min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.6 min (fixture) | 0.240 mL | 2.40 mL | **6.0 min** |
| 0.525 min (`method.csv`) | 0.210 mL | 2.10 mL | 5.25 min |

Every programme below ends with a **6.2–6.4 min** hold at its starting %B after the
return step. That is the only change from the programmes of the original runs.

Same column, flow (0.4 mL/min), temperature (45 °C), injection volume (10 µL), diluent
(50:50 water:ACN) and sample as the original runs of each dataset.

## 4 · How E1 will be read

Δ = predicted − measured, mean over the four peaks; positive means the engine over-predicts.

| mean Δ for E1 | axis of the residual | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| ≈ +0.012 min (run 3's) | **φ0** | diagnostic 7 (φ0 departure, #44) stands on the right variable; the Δφ0 ≥ 10 %B tier is re-pinned by #55 |
| ≈ +0.028 min (run 4's) | **Δφ / s\*** | the residual is s\*-mediated after all; #44's split between diagnostics 1 and 7 is re-examined on #55 |
| in between, with the E4 spread ≥ 0.008 min | undecided | the repeatability floor is too high for one injection; E1 needs replicates |

The boundary is 0.020 min. E4's replicates say whether 0.016 min of separation is real on
this instrument; the literature figure standing in for it today is ±0.002 min.

the repeatability floor *at 6.7 CV*, and E1's Δ is read under the same re-equilibration as
run 3 and run 4, which is what §4's comparison needs; it does not test the re-equilibration
mechanism itself.
